Common questions

How much does window cleaning cost?

Price depends on your property size and how often you'd like us to visit. Message us on WhatsApp with your address, or a couple of photos, and we'll come back to you with an exact, free quote for a regular round.

What is water-fed pole window cleaning?

It's a modern cleaning method using purified water delivered through a telescopic pole, cleaned from ground level. Because the water is purified, windows dry completely spot- and streak-free without any need for a chemical or a squeegee finish — and there's no ladder risk.

How often should I get my windows cleaned?

Most customers choose a 4- or 8-weekly round. Properties near main roads or with a lot of surrounding trees often benefit from the more frequent 4-weekly option.

Do I need to be home?

No. As long as we can access your windows, you don't need to be in. Payment is handled by direct debit for regular customers, so there's nothing to arrange on the day.

Which areas do you cover?

We're based in Tyldesley and regularly clean in Wigan, Leigh, Bolton, Atherton, Astley, Boothstown, Walkden, Westhoughton and Hindley. Message us if you're just outside this area — we can often still help.
